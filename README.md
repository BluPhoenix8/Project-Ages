# Project Ages

A Minecraft 1.21.1 modpack for NeoForge, by BluPhoenix8. You work your way up through the
ages, from the Stone Age to Space, with quests to guide you.

## Download

- **Modpack:** on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/project-ages), or `Project-Ages-0.7.1.zip` from the
  [latest release](https://github.com/BluPhoenix8/Project-Ages/releases/latest). Open it in the CurseForge app, or in
  Prism Launcher (Add Instance, then Import).
- **Server:** `Project-Ages-Server-0.7.1.zip` from the
  [latest release](https://github.com/BluPhoenix8/Project-Ages/releases/latest). Unzip it and double-click
  "Project Ages Server" (Linux or Mac: `./panel/start.sh`). The server panel walks you through
  setup and downloads the mods from CurseForge on the first start.

## What's in here

`pack/` holds the pack itself: configs, scripts, quests, and one small file per mod saying where
it downloads from. There are no mod files here: they always come from CurseForge or Modrinth.
